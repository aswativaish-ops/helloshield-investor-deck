How this list was compiled

Reviewed the accessible HelloShield chat index, relevant recent and selected older turns, and the inbound/outbound requirements and progress records. This is a consolidated inventory of scenarios found, not a claim that every message in every historical chat was read. Older proposals are separated from current scope; chats and records can describe different branches or deployments.
